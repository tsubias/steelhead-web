## Plain English Summary

- Steelhead helps you book jobs while you’re busy working.
- We collect information from you and from the customers who interact with Steelhead.
- We use that information **only** to run and improve the Steelhead service.
- We share data only with the providers needed to make the service work (currently: Twilio, the AI provider, Google Calendar).
- We **never sell** your data or your customers’ data.
- You can request access, correction, or deletion of your information.
- This service is for USA-based business use only.

## 1\. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Steelhead (“we,” “us,” or “the App”) collects, uses, and shares information about you (the “contractor” or “User”) and your customers (the “Customer”) whose calls are handled through the service.

## 2\. Information We Collect

1. Information you provide: name, location, Apple/Google app authentication, service hours, calendar connection, and account settings.
2. Information from customer interactions: customer name, address, phone number, call content, transcripts, and appointment details.
3. Automatically collected: call metadata, usage data, device information, and approximate location data used for scheduling and location-related functionality.
4. Permissions you grant: calendar access and call-related permissions.

## 3\. How We Use Information

We use the information solely to:

- Answer and handle inbound calls on your dedicated Steelhead account.
- Manage appointments on your connected calendar.
- Generate transcripts and log entries.
- Improve the AI agent and overall service (using de-identified data where possible).
- Send service-related notifications.
- Maintain security and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it to 3rd parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

## 4\. Third-Party Service Providers

We share information only as needed with:

- Twilio: for your dedicated Steelhead phone number and call handling.
- OpenAI (or successor AI provider): for the conversational agent.
- Google: for calendar integration.
- Hosting, notification, and other infrastructure providers.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and only for the purpose of delivering the Steelhead service. We prefer USA-based processing when feasible.

## 5\. Call Handling and Your Dedicated Number

Each User is provisioned with a dedicated Twilio number. The User may set up conditional call forwarding from their existing business number to this dedicated number. By using the App, you consent to AI processing of call content, generation of transcripts, and creation of calendar events for the purpose of providing the service.

## 6\. Data Retention

We retain account and operational data while your account is active. Call transcripts and log entries are typically retained for 6 months, after which they may be archived or deleted. You may request earlier deletion of specific records.

## 7\. Your Rights Under U.S. State Privacy Laws

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of personal information (subject to legal exceptions).
- Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. (As we do not engage in the sale or sharing of information except as needed to provide the service or as required by law, you’ll be asking for something we already do.).

To exercise these rights, contact us at [support@steelhead.ai](mailto:support@steelhead.ai) or through in-app support. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

## 8\. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No system is completely secure. Call forwarding and VoIP transmission carry inherent risks.

## 9\. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18\.

## 10\. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance. Material changes will be communicated via the App or email.
